title: Voice typing, your way
date: 2026-08-12
---

Dictation now works the way you want it to, wherever you're typing into Remem.

- **Choice of engine** — stick with the on-device model for fully offline, private transcription, or switch to Chrome's built-in speech recognition for instant results with no download.
- **Opt-in from Settings** — turn voice typing on when you want it, with a clear download-progress indicator the first time the on-device model is fetched.
- **Available everywhere you write** — Memory Builder chat and your story bank both get the same mic button now.
- **Lighter install** — trimmed the extension package by about a fifth, so it downloads and updates faster.

As always, the on-device engine never sends your voice off this device.
