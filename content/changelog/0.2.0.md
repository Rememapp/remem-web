The Memory Builder is now the fastest way to teach Remem about yourself.

- **Chat-based capture** — answer natural questions, and confirm each extracted fact before it's saved.
- **Voice dictation** — talk through your employment history instead of typing it.
- **CV upload** — drop in a résumé and review the extracted facts one by one.
- **Conflict resolution** — when a new fact contradicts an old one, you choose which wins.

Everything captured stays on your device, as always.
