title: Certifications, smarter uploads, and beta
date: 2026-08-15

Remem is moving out of alpha and into public beta, alongside a few upgrades to how it captures your background.

- **Certifications section** — courses, credentials, and licenses now get their own place in your profile, right alongside experience and education.
- **Smarter document detection** — uploads are now classified by what's actually in them, not just the filename, so a certificate or cover letter lands in the right slot even with a generic name like "download.pdf."
- **Improve wording, without losing your voice** — the button formerly called Rephrase now saves its polished version alongside your original story answer instead of replacing it, so Remem keeps learning how you actually write.
- **Clearer autofill status** — status updates now read "Scanning Fields" and "Crafting Answers" instead of vault-speak.

Still pre-1.0, so expect the occasional rough edge — keep the feedback coming.
