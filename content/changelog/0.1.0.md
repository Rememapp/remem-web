The first end-to-end build of Remem, in the hands of early testers.

- **Local profile storage** — encrypted, on-device memory for your personal information.
- **Smart form detection** — field mapping across Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and Ashby.
- **Review-then-fill** — every suggestion shown before a single field changes.
- **Hard no-auto-submit rule** — the submit button is untouchable, by design.
